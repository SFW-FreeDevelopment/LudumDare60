# LD entry copy — Charlotte's Balloonatic

Review copy for the Ludum Dare 60 **Jam** entry, theme **Old Tech**. The WebGL archive has index.html
at its root. Add the final playable hosting URL when Steven publishes the entry.

## Blurb

Charlotte and her bunny have dreams to deliver. Take their pink hot-air balloon through floating
islands: a bottle for the nursery, a lost teddy for the Toy Isles, and a letter for the Cloud Garden.

There is no rudder. Hold the burner to rise, let the air cool to settle, and find the breeze that
carries you home. Two sandbags can help with a difficult approach. Three short flights, a little wind
reading, and a gentle landing lead to a moonlit journey home. *For Charlotte ♥*

## How to play

- Hold **Space** or the left mouse button to heat; release early to cool and descend.
- Press **B** or right mouse to drop a sandbag for extra lift / gentler descent.
- Choose altitude to choose wind. The ladder and windsocks show direction and strength.
- Land gently on the recipient's timber deck; cargo is picked up at launch and carried visibly.
- Breeze Perches let you rest: descend onto one with heat released, then hold Space to lift off.
- **Enter** or the result button opens the voyage map; **R** restarts during flight.
- **Escape** pauses. Losing focus pauses safely; resume when ready.

Keyboard and mouse play. Settings include separate music/effects volume, Wind Assist and Reduced
Motion. Browser settings and best ratings are remembered locally. Missing an island is recoverable.

## Theme

Old Tech is the balloon's machinery: a hand-fired burner, temperature gauge, rope and wicker, sandbags,
wooden docks and windsocks. Horizontal movement belongs to the wind; choosing altitude is the steering.
The visual style follows the supplied cozy pixel-art concept: rounded pink fabric, warm timber,
Charlotte and her bunny, blue cloud depth, toy blocks, flowers and a windmill.
