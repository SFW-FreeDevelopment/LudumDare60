# LD entry copy — Charlotte's Balloonatic (LD60, theme: Old Tech)

Paste-ready text for the ldjam.com entry page. The playable build is the zip from `npm run zip`
(uploads as the web play — `index.html` is at the archive root).

## Blurb

A baby named Charlotte crosses a sky of floating islands in an old-fashioned hot-air balloon — no
engine, no rudder, no steering lever. Heat to rise, cooling to sink, two sandbags for emergencies,
and the wind at whatever altitude you choose as your only way to go anywhere. Deliver her home;
then chart the next island on the voyage map.

Three courses teach the machine: Nursery Skies shows you the burner, Toy Isles teaches that the
middle air sprints while the upper air rebukes you, Cloud Garden dares you to cross on a fast
ceiling through storm banks, gulls, and floating rocks. Par times rate every delivery with stars.

## Theme note

"Old Tech" is the balloon's machinery, not a screen filter: the burner you feather by hand, the
temperature gauge with a live flame, rope and wicker, sandbags as the only brake, windsocks and
storm-chart reading. There are no left/right controls because a balloon has no rudder — going
somewhere is a negotiation with the wind, and the whole game is choosing the altitude you argue at.
The visuals are 16-bit clean (deliberately no CRT/scanline/phosphor), tuned against a motion-sickness
floor — and a Reduced Motion setting honors it.

## How to play

- **Space** or hold left mouse — burner; let go — cool and sink
- **B** / right mouse — drop a sandbag (two total; they are the brake for landing)
- Land gently on the delivery island (the windsock shows the local wind)
- **R** after a delivery opens the voyage chart; **Enter** flies on
- **Esc** pauses; **R** after a bad landing flies the same sky again

Fully keyboard-playable. Settings: music/sfx volume, wind assist, reduced motion.
