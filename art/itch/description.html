Join Charlotte and her bunny in a pink hot-air balloon above a sea of clouds. Bring a bottle to the nursery, a lost teddy to the Toy Isles, and a letter to the Cloud Garden.

🎈 Float. Explore. Deliver dreams.

There is no rudder. Warm the balloon to rise, let it cool to settle, and find the breeze that carries you home. Read the windsocks, rest on a Breeze Perch, and make a gentle landing on the timber deck.

🧸 Your tiny flight manual

  • Hold Space / left mouse: heat the balloon and rise. Release early to cool and descend.
  • B / right mouse: drop one of your two sandbags for extra lift.
  • Choose your altitude: each wind layer carries you differently. Watch the wind ladder.
  • Land gently: slide along the destination deck to bring your parcel home.
  • Breeze Perches: descend onto one with heat released to park; hold Space to lift off again.
  • Escape: pause. R: restart your flight. Enter: continue after a delivery.
